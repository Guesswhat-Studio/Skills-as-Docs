Agent skills need team governance before they become team memory.

Skills are easy to create, share, and install.

But teams need a harder answer:

Which skills should become trusted shared capabilities?

Useful is not enough.

Teams need to know who owns a skill, what changed, what was reviewed, where it can run, and how to roll it back.

That is the claim behind **Skills Charter**:

> Treat every distributed skill as a candidate until its charter is approved.

Skills Charter is a Git-backed team governance workflow for agent skills. It helps teams move skills from personal shortcuts to reviewed, approved, and auditable team capabilities.
