The team-scale breakage

The current maintenance model breaks once skills leave one person's machine.

Teams have useful instructions everywhere. The issue is that those instructions are not governed as shared operational assets.

Positioning

Skills Charter owns the human governance layer.

We are not the standard, marketplace, installer, or runtime. We are the reviewable source of truth before those layers consume a skill.
